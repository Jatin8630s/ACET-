About The Institution

Leading the Frontier of Technical & Professional Education

Aligarh College of Engineering and Technology (ACET) was established in 2001 under the visionary leadership of eminent educationists. Operating alongside the Aligarh College of Pharmacy (Estd. 2006), our sprawling 15-acre green eco-campus at Mathura Bypass Road, Sasni Gate, Aligarh provides an inspiring educational environment.

Approved by AICTE & PCI New Delhi, and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU Lucknow, Code: 096) and BTE UP, ACET is dedicated to developing industry-ready engineers, pharmacists, managers, and software professionals.
